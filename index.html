Mahmoods AMS is an Android application designed to support academic attendance management and related academic activities for authorized students and college staff.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features used and the institution's configuration, the application may process account information such as email addresses and account identifiers, student or staff identifiers, academic information, timetable data, and attendance records.
